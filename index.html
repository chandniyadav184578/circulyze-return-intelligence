The problem

Fixed rules don't fit two very different returns.

Most retailers still route electronics returns with static rules and spreadsheets. That works until it doesn't — a barely-used laptop and a cracked phone end up in the same bucket, and both value and materials leak out along the way.

Fixed rules→ Inconsistent returns→ Lost value→ Unnecessary waste

Two returned products, same category, can need completely different decisions — one belongs on the resale shelf, the other belongs in a recycler's hands.
